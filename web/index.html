SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

From grocery sales to a next-day forecast

01 · DataSpark joins daily sales by store and product family to Favorita’s store metadata. City and province identify a store’s location. Some products sell by weight, so unit sales can be fractional.

02 · FeaturesEach store-family series supplies its own lagged sales and trailing averages. Inputs also include calendar features, store format and the planned number of items on promotion.

03 · SelectionFour Spark regressors train on data through April 2017. Lowest May–June validation RMSE selects the winner. July–15 August is reserved for a rolling one-day-ahead test against last week’s sales.

04 · ServingFastAPI reconstructs the same features from strictly earlier dates and uses the saved winner for single forecasts and CSV batches. The next supported date after the labeled history is 16 August 2017.
